# Launch plan: Pro price increase to $25

## Objective
Increase Pro plan revenue by raising the price from $20 to $25 per month while minimizing churn.

## Plan
1. **Value messaging.** Explain the increase in terms of new features and improvements shipped over the past year.
2. **Advance notice.** Email all Pro customers two weeks before the change and show an in-app banner.
3. **Grandfathering.** Offer existing customers the current price for 3 months as a thank-you.
4. **Update billing and pricing page** on launch day.
5. **Prepare support.** Write an FAQ and canned responses for the support team.
6. **Monitor.** Watch churn, conversion and support tickets daily for the first month.

## Timeline
- Week -2: announcement email and banner
- Week 0: price change live
- Week +4: review results

## Risks
- Higher churn than expected. Mitigation: retention offers for customers who try to cancel.
- Negative social media reaction. Mitigation: clear, honest messaging.

## Success metrics
- Churn stays within 1 percentage point of baseline.
- Pro MRR increases by at least 15%.
