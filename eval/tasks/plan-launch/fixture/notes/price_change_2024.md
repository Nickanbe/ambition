# Retro: Pro price change, March 2024 ($15 -> $20)

Written by: growth team, 2024-05-20

## What we did
- Email to all Pro customers on 2024-03-01, new price on invoices from 2024-03-15 (14 days notice).
- Applied to everyone, including customers who had signed up in February.
- No in-app notice. Pricing page updated on 2024-03-15.

## What happened
Monthly Pro logo churn, month after the change vs the 3 months before:

| Tenure at change | Customers | Churn before (avg/mo) | Churn month after |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-1 months | 212 | 6.0% | 18.5% |
| 2-3 months | 301 | 3.9% | 5.1% |
| 4-12 months | 655 | 2.2% | 2.6% |
| 13+ months | 480 | 1.3% | 1.4% |
| **All Pro** | 1,648 | 2.4% | 4.1% |

About 70% of the excess churn came from customers in their first month or two. Exit survey: "I just signed up at $15 and it already went up" was the top reason.
Churn returned to baseline by month 3. Net revenue was up 19% after 6 months.

## Also
- 9 EU customers complained the notice was too short; legal said we were lucky (see terms excerpt).
- Annual customers were not touched (they renewed at the new price), which went fine.
- We didn't track downgrades to Starter separately; finance thinks some revenue loss was downgrades, not churn.
